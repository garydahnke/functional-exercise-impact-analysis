# functional-exercise-impact-analysis
This repository contains an analysis of incorporating functional exercises into my weight training exercise programs and its impact on me. 

I have been weight training for 42 years. Recently, I have come across functional exercises and began to understand their purpose. While my weight training keeps me strong, I need to find exercises that improves my mobility and flexibility so that my tendons and joints and strengthen as well. To start, I have added pull ups, Controlled Articular Rotations (CAR), and tendon strengthening exercises to determine the impacts on my body and my weight training.

I weight train each muscle group once per week and follow a low-volume, high-intensity strategy for my weight training exercises. I limit each execise to 2 set from 5 to 12 repetitions pushing myself to 0 to 1 repetions of failure. By limiting myself to 2 sets, my mental focus and energy to weight training is much stronger yielding better lifts and keeping my enthusiasm to lift high.

I have created a spreadsheets that I will use to track and collect data. This spreadsheet currently contains 5 sheets and defined as follows:
1. Workouts - This sheet is used to log all weight training, CAR/Mobility, and tendon strengthening exercises.
2. Daily Health Log - This sheet is used to track daily personal health markers including objective statistics and subjective measurements based on personal judgment.
3. Assessments - This sheet contains activities, exercise, and other objective statistic that will be taken weekly or monthly to track progress to evaluate the impact
                 of functional exercises on my health and weight training.
5. Exercises - This sheet contains a list of exerices and associated information. 
6. Column Index - This sheet contains a definition of each column name and the sheet the column is used.

Note that columns may be added or removed from any sheet to improve the process of collecting data for analysis.
